08/18/2026 Meteomatics Wins Citatio’s Best Weather App 2026 Award The Meteomatics Weather App has won the Best Weather App 2026 award from Citatio , an independent platform that ranks products using public customer reviews. Citatio compared five weather apps for its 2026 ranking and scored each on how positively reviewers rated it across quality, value for money, delivery, customer service, and ordering & software. Meteomatics finished first with a composite score of 73%, ahead of Tomorrow.io (63%), Foreca (59%), meteoblue (32%) and AccuWeather (11%).
