10/13/2026 Primeo Energie Automates Solar Forecasting with EURO1k to Cut Balancing Costs Primeo Energie, a Swiss energy supplier that manages around 1 GW of installed PV capacity, distributed over ten thousands of small assets, rebuilt its forecasting system around Meteomatics' high-resolution EURO1k model. More frequent updates across far more sites, combined with EURO1k's strong performance on solar radiation, have cut forecast errors by 40%, saving millions of Swiss francs a year.
