06/11/2025 EURO1k Outperforms MEPS in 70% of Cases, NORCE Evaluation Shows A recent evaluation by NORCE as part of Project Gondul shows that EURO1k , Meteomatics’ high-resolution weather model, delivers more accurate forecasts than the MEPS model from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute in over 70% of cases for key weather parameters. This result underscores the potential of EURO1k to substantially improve decision-making in critical operational environments, particularly in cold, Arctic conditions.
